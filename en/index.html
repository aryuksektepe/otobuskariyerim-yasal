Privacy Policy

My Bus Career · Last updated: October 30, 2026

This text explains which of your personal data are processed, and for what purposes, when you play My Bus Career (Turkish title: Otobüs Kariyerim), and what rights you have over that data. Under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), this text is also the disclosure notice.

In short

The game does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location. Your game progress stays on your device and is not sent to us. If iCloud Backup is on for your device, your save is included in your device backup in your Apple account. The game has no server of its own. The external services that process data are Google AdMob, for rewarded and interstitial ads, and Apple, for purchases. Personalised ads are shown only if you allow them.

Data controller

Data controller: Ali Rıza Yüksektepe. You can send your requests and questions to support@odexi.app.

Data kept on your device

Money, buses, drivers, tasks, tokens, settings and other game progress are stored in your device's storage and are not sent to us. If iCloud Backup is on for your device, your game save is included in your device backup in your Apple account. If you set up your device, or a new iPhone, from that backup, your save comes back too. Apple provides these services through your account, and we have no access to this data. The game does not send game data off the device.

Notifications

Reminders such as shift right refills, offline earnings or mission renewals are scheduled locally on your device. No data is sent to a server for these notifications. You can turn notifications off in Settings in the game, or in your device settings.

Ads (Google AdMob)

When you watch a rewarded ad, or an interstitial ad is shown, the ad is served through Google AdMob. While an ad is shown, Google processes your IP address, device model, operating system and app version, your interaction with the ad, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. If you allow it, your advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, the advertising ID on Android) is also used to select ads that suit you. If you do not allow it, non-personalised ads are shown. For these ads, the technical data listed above is still used for display, measurement and fraud prevention. You can find out how Google processes this data at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases (App Store)

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store, so your payment details do not reach us. Unlocking purchased products on your device and restoring them are carried out using transaction records that Apple verifies on the device. In this process, no name, email address or store account information is sent to us. Apple keeps purchase records for as long as tax and consumer law requires.

Purposes and legal bases

Delivering purchases and restoring them falls under Article 5(2)(c) of KVKK (establishing and performing a contract). Preventing ad abuse and showing non-personalised ads falls under Article 5(2)(f) of KVKK (legitimate interest). Personalised ads and the use of your advertising ID are based on your explicit consent under Article 5(1) of KVKK. Not giving consent does not stop you from using the game.

Changing your ad preference

Before the first ad is shown, the game asks whether you allow personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings. In the European Union, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, this question is asked through Google's consent window. On iOS, you can also turn off tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your device. You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

Transfers abroad

Data is transferred to Google LLC (AdMob) for advertising, and to Apple for payment processing. These companies' servers are located outside Turkey, mainly in the United States of America. Transfers abroad are made through the routes provided for in Article 9 of KVKK: appropriate safeguards provided by the service provider, or the occasional cases under Article 9(6), where the transfer is necessary for the performance of a contract or you have given your explicit consent.

Retention period

Game data on your device is kept until you delete the app or reset your save in Settings. Google's retention of advertising data is subject to Google's policies. Apple keeps purchase records under its own policies. Requests you send to us are kept for the legal periods after they have been answered.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the game data on your device. The Reset save option in Settings replaces the save on your device with a new game save. You can delete the copy in your device backup from your iCloud account. You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings. If you have sent us a request, you can write to support@odexi.app to ask for it to be deleted.

Children

The game is not designed for children under 13, and we knowingly do not collect data from that age group. Users under 18 must obtain the consent of a parent or guardian before making any purchase.

Security

Because game data is not sent to a server, we do not keep a database of it. Connections to ad and store services are made over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Your rights under KVKK

Under Article 11 of KVKK, you have the right to: find out whether your personal data is processed; request information about it if it has been processed; learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used in line with that purpose; know the third parties to whom it is transferred; ask for it to be corrected if it is incomplete or inaccurate, or to have it deleted or destroyed; ask for the third parties to whom it was transferred to be notified of these actions; object to a result against you arising from analysis of your data by automated systems alone; and claim compensation if you suffer loss because of unlawful processing.

Making a request

Send your requests to support@odexi.app. Your request must include the information needed to verify your identity and state what you are asking for. Requests are answered free of charge within 30 days at the latest. If you find the answer insufficient, or do not receive a reply in time, you can still complain to the Turkish Data Protection Board.

Changes

This text is updated when the game or the services it uses change. The date of the last update appears at the top of the page.
